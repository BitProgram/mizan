# MizanZarb

> The scale for your money.

A local-first, open-source money OS for solo software developers.
Know your MRR, burn, runway, and break-even — without a cloud account.

## What it will do

- Normalize MRR across monthly, annual, and lifetime revenue
- Separate COGS from fixed costs (real gross margin)
- Calculate runway, break-even MRR, and 30-day forecast
- Track unit economics: LTV, CAC, payback, churn
- Compute effective hourly rate per product
